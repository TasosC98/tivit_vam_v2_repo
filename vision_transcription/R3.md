# R3: the paper-1 experiments on a second dataset (branch `KeyWise-R3`)

Goal: train KeyWise on R3 from scratch with exactly the paper-1 recipe, so that R3
gets the same baseline as PianoVAM (E1, three seeds, compared with V2N's released
predictions), then the transfer and ablation runs. Everything here was checked on
2026-11-17 against the public release; the server steps below repeat the checks on
the downloaded copy.

## 1. The dataset

R3 (Zivanovic, Pilkov, Cancino-Chacón, *Pay Attention to the Keys*, IJCAI 2025,
arXiv 2411.09037). Raw release: archive.org/details/r3dataset_raw, CC BY-NC-ND
(the readme says CC BY-NC-SA); cite doi 10.5281/zenodo.15921293. Two pianists,
62 practice sessions (= 62 dates), silent pianos (Yamaha GB1K grand, Essex EUP-116
upright), top-down GoPro.

| folder | subset | split | chunks | hours | notes (PPAN Table 1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `r3s_train` | R3s (Rachmaninoff 3rd concerto, one pianist) | train | 219 | 8.54 | 292,814 |
| `r3s_test` | R3s | test | 74 | 2.92 | 98,045 |
| `train` | R3x (varied repertoire, both pianists) | train | 495 | 15.81 | 479,629 |
| `test` | R3x | test | 107 | 3.64 | 119,725 |

* Files per chunk: `<session>_full_split<N>.mp4` (video with its own audio),
  `<session>_split<N>.mid` (labels), `rach3_…flac` (audio, unused). All 895
  video/MIDI pairs match by this rule. A recording here is one chunk; its id is the
  MIDI stem (e.g. `rach3_2021-04-13_a02_split1`), which is also the file name of
  V2N's released R3 predictions.
* Video: every file is 1920×1080 (archive metadata). The two chunks opened locally
  are 30 fps with MIDI starting at 0 and ending with the video (28.97/28.98 s,
  49.67/49.69 s). `check_data --probe` checks all 895 on the server.
* **Geometry.** The camera hangs over the piano and faces the pianist, so every
  frame is the usual view turned by 180°: A0 is at the image's RIGHT and the black
  keys are BELOW the white ones (checked on one frame of each of the 143 sessions).
  The boxes (`r3s_bounding_boxes.json`, `r3x_bounding_boxes.json`, one per session)
  are `x1 y1 x2 y2` in the frame straightened by turning it clockwise by
  `rot_angle` about its centre (−1° … 3°); the other sign misses the keyboard on
  the 3° sessions. The loader turns the box back into the raw frame and orders the
  corners for the 180° view (`data.orientation: rot180`). Verified by drawing the
  MIDI's held keys on warped frames of two chunks (grand and upright): the marks sit
  under the fingers; with a mirrored order they do not.
* **The official boxes are too coarse for KeyWise** (found 2026-11-20). One rotated
  rectangle per session (84 distinct over 144 sessions), integer pixels, no
  perspective. KeyWise reads key k from column k of the strip, so a box off by about
  half a key moves every note by one key: the first run with the official boxes
  (`r3_full`, R3E1) scored R3s On@50 26.7 vs V2N 78.8, and `offset_scan` showed why
  -- our notes are right but one key too LOW on 56/74 R3s test chunks (F1 77.8 when
  shifted back) and one key too HIGH on 16/107 R3x test chunks; timing is right
  (best lag 0). V2N (one global model) is not affected. `check_alignment` cannot see
  it (sessions with the same check numbers have different shifts). Fix: the four
  corners of every session set by hand, with the model's key grid on the warped
  strip (`corners.py export` + `scripts/corner_tool.html` -> `configs/r3_corners.json`,
  `data.r3_corners`). Labels are not used; train, valid and test are done the same way.
  Done 2026-11-20 for all 144 sessions (~1.5 h by hand; same-box sessions start
  from the previous session's corners) -> `configs/r3_corners_hand.json`. The ends
  were right (black-key fit A0 +0.10 ± 0.13, C8 +0.18 ± 0.15 white keys, worst 0.48;
  official boxes +0.20 ± 0.31 / +0.05 ± 0.31, worst 1.23), but a per-octave check
  found the MIDDLE of the keyboard 0.3-0.55 white keys off on 17 sessions (4 of them
  test): a few px of error in a front corner gives the homography a perspective along
  the keyboard that the scene does not have; the ends stay pinned, the middle bows.
  Fix: `corners.py refine` moves every session's corners by a few px (median 1, max
  7.7) so that its black keys and white-key gaps (two heights: they stay vertical) sit
  where the median session has them along the whole keyboard; worst octave block per
  session median 0.15 -> 0.00 white keys, > 0.3 in 17 -> 0 sessions (checked by eye at
  4x on the worst ones). `rach3_2021-08-19_a01` (train) is kept as set by hand: its
  picture has hands over the middle in every sampled frame. Result:
  `configs/r3_corners.json`. `configs/r3c.yaml` = `r3.yaml` + these corners, with its
  own strip cache; its runs are `r3c_*` (section 5). `r3_full` and `r3_full_s2`
  (official boxes) stay as the "official boxes" result. Paper: corners set by hand
  once per session (as PianoVAM's) and refined on the key pattern (no labels); V2N
  used the official boxes.
* **Labels**: silent-piano MIDI, one track, sustain pedal as CC64 only. Note-off =
  key release, as PianoVAM's `key_offset` and V2N's default R3 scoring; pedal is
  ignored. No note-on while the key is still down in the two chunks checked.
  Many very short notes: 21 % of the notes of the Rachmaninoff chunk last less than
  one video frame (33 ms), 37 % less than 50 ms (upright chunk: 4.5 % / 13 %).
  Onsets are unaffected; offsets of such notes are below the frame rate.
* **Sync** (V2N, arXiv 2608.03419, `r3_package/sync_analysis.json`): 70 of 895
  chunks are > 200 ms out of sync: 10 R3x **test** chunks (`ls_2024-02-06_a02` and
  `_a03`, MIDI 0.73–0.86 s early), 60 train chunks in 13 sessions, none in R3s test.
  V2N and PPAN train on them uncorrected, and so does our baseline. V2N reports R3x
  with and without the 10 test chunks: `configs/r3_nosync.yaml` gives the second
  line (never instead of the full test set).
* **Valid**: R3 has none. Per subset, whole recording dates move from train to
  valid (10 %, `valid_seed` 1234), never a date with an out-of-sync session:
  R3s 198 train / 21 valid / 74 test, R3x 439 / 56 / 107 chunks; 21.8 h train,
  2.6 h valid, 6.6 h test. V2N and PPAN train on all 714 train chunks; we train on
  637 (say so in the paper).

## 2. What is the same as paper 1, what differs

`configs/r3.yaml` is `vam_full.yaml` (E1) with a new `data:` block — same model,
warp (1408×112), labels (30 fps, key releases), augmentation, schedule (25 epochs,
cosine), checkpoint choice (`onset_f1_best` on valid), threshold calibration on
valid (`full_f1`) and metrics. A test (`tests/test_r3.py`) fails if this ever drifts.
One deliberate difference: `keyboard.decode_height: 1080` (native). R3's keyboard
fills ~45 % of the frame width (PianoVAM ~90 %), so at 1080p it gets ~880 px, close
to PianoVAM's ~1150 px at 720p; at 720p it would get ~590.
`configs/r3c.yaml` (all runs from 2026-11-20 on) is `r3.yaml` + the hand-set
corners (`data.r3_corners`) and its own cache folder; nothing else differs.

## 3. Code changes on this branch

* `metadata.py`: `data.format: r3` (`load_recordings_r3`, `r3_box_corners`),
  `Recording.subset/session`, valid split by date.
* `labels.py`: R3 labels are MIDI (`MIDI_LABEL_FORMATS`); `compare_midi`,
  `check_data` (counts per subset, valid sessions) follow.
* `evaluate.py` prints means per subset (R3s, R3x) and writes a `subset` column;
  `score_midi.py` prints a full table + paired Wilcoxon per subset;
  `paper/compare_runs.py --subset r3s|r3x --exclude …`.
* `scripts/evaluate_run.sh`: `OVERRIDES="…"` for calibrate + evaluate (cross-dataset).
* `video.SequentialWarpedVideo` + `data.cache_reader: cv2`: the R3 cache is decoded by
  OpenCV front to back (same frame plan, corners, warp, RGB; < 100 MB per worker);
  `build_cache --compare <id>` checks it against an existing cache; caches made by the
  other reader are rebuilt. PianoVAM / PianoYT keep decord.
* Hand-set corners: `corners.py export` (one median picture per session + the
  official corners), `scripts/corner_tool.html` (browser, no install: corners, zooms,
  the strip with the model's key grid), `corners.py refine` (key pattern lined up
  along the whole keyboard), `metadata.load_r3_corners` + `data.r3_corners`
  (every session must then have corners). `offset_scan.py`: lag / pitch shift / drift
  of predicted vs reference notes (how the one-key shift was found).
* Configs `r3.yaml` (official boxes), `r3c.yaml` (hand-set corners), `r3c_vel.yaml`,
  `r3c_global88.yaml`, `r3c_finetune.yaml`, `r3_nosync.yaml` (scoring only, serves
  both); corners `configs/r3_corners.json`; job lists `scripts/jobs_r3.txt`,
  `scripts/jobs_r3_ablations.txt`.
* PianoVAM / PianoYT behaviour is unchanged (all 78 tests pass).

## 4. Runbook (GPU server)

```bash
# 0. code + environment (every new SSH login: the activate line)
cd ~/tivit_vam_v2_repo/vision_transcription && git fetch && git checkout KeyWise-R3 && git pull
source .venv/bin/activate
python -m pytest tests/test_r3.py -q                    # 14 passed
nvidia-smi; df -h /raid_storage                         # GPU free? ~100 GB free for the cache?

# 1. the download: four folders + two box files, 895 chunks
R=/raid_storage/data_achatzigiannis/R3_raw/r3dataset_raw   # the archive.org download's own folder
for d in r3s_train r3s_test train test; do echo "$d $(ls $R/$d/*.mid | wc -l) mid $(ls $R/$d/*.mp4 | wc -l) mp4"; done
#   expect 219/219, 74/74, 495/495, 107/107

# 2. dataset check (counts per subset, valid sessions, missing files), then every video
python -m pianovam_vision.check_data --config configs/r3.yaml
python -m pianovam_vision.check_data --config configs/r3.yaml --probe > results/r3_probe.txt 2>&1
tail -n 25 results/r3_probe.txt                         # fps, resolutions, hours, issues

# 3. V2N's released R3 predictions, scored with OUR code (no GPU): must reproduce
#    V2N's table (R3s On 78.8/91.7, ++Off 69.5/88.9; R3x 73.3/86.6, 68.5/85.2)
git clone --depth 1 https://github.com/yonghyunk1m/V2N.git ~/V2N_latest
mkdir -p ~/V2N_r3_test && cp ~/V2N_latest/predicted_midi/r3s_test/*.mid ~/V2N_latest/predicted_midi/r3x_test/*.mid ~/V2N_r3_test/
ls ~/V2N_r3_test | wc -l                                # 181
python -m pianovam_vision.score_midi --config configs/r3.yaml --split test \
    --pred ~/V2N_r3_test --csv results/V2N_r3_scores.csv > results/V2N_r3_scores.txt 2>&1
grep -A6 "^subset\|^mean over" results/V2N_r3_scores.txt

# 3b. keyboard corners by hand, then refined (the official boxes are too coarse, see
#     section 1). DONE 2026-11-20: configs/r3_corners_hand.json -> configs/r3_corners.json
#     (used by configs/r3c.yaml). To redo:
#     server: one picture per session (median of frames: no hands) + official corners
python -m pianovam_vision.corners export --config configs/r3.yaml --out ~/r3_corner_frames
#     PC: copy ~/r3_corner_frames, open scripts/corner_tool.html in a browser, choose the
#     folder, set the corners of every session, download r3_corners.json and save it as
#     configs/r3_corners_hand.json; then (PC or server, a few minutes):
python -m pianovam_vision.corners refine --frames ~/r3_corner_frames \
    --corners configs/r3_corners_hand.json --out configs/r3_corners.json --keep rach3_2021-08-19_a01

# 4. strip cache (~3.3 M frames, ~85 GB at quality 85; ~4 h with 8 workers).
#    DONE for r3.yaml (official boxes); for r3c.yaml see 5b.
#    R3 is 1080p HEVC: built with OpenCV front to back (data.cache_reader: cv2), because
#    decord takes ~7 GB of RAM per video and failed on 373/895 videos with 8 workers.
#    Caches from decord are rebuilt. OpenCV gives the same frames as decord:
python -m pianovam_vision.build_cache --config configs/r3.yaml --compare rach3_2022-07-19_a02_split2
#    (shift +0 near zero, every other shift clearly larger)
CONFIGS=configs/r3.yaml GB_PER_CACHE=100 QUALITY=85 bash scripts/build_caches.sh
tail -f logs/build_caches.log                           # until "all caches done"
python -m pianovam_vision.build_cache --config configs/r3.yaml --check

# 5. geometry on every chunk: A0..C8 fit of the black-key pattern (from the cache),
#    plus pictures of a few chunks with the labels drawn on (check the marks sit on
#    pressed keys). Same commands with configs/r3c.yaml after 5b.
python -m pianovam_vision.check_alignment --config configs/r3.yaml --csv results/alignment_r3.csv \
    > results/alignment_r3.txt 2>&1; tail -n 15 results/alignment_r3.txt
python -m pianovam_vision.preview --config configs/r3.yaml --record_time rach3_2021-04-13_a02_split1 --snap_to_onset --time 20 --out_dir preview/r3
python -m pianovam_vision.preview --config configs/r3.yaml --record_time ls_2024-02-06_a02_split1 --snap_to_onset --time 20 --out_dir preview/r3
#    one frame of EVERY chunk (895), 24 per sheet: boxes that drift within a session show here
python -m pianovam_vision.draw_keyboard --config configs/r3.yaml --sheet 24 --show_active --out_dir preview/r3_sheet

# 5b. the cache with the hand-set corners (own folder; the corners are in each video's
#     meta, so the two caches cannot be mixed up). Same quality as the first one. With 4
#     workers it can run next to a training (~125 MB of RAM per worker; ~8 h).
python -m pianovam_vision.check_data --config configs/r3c.yaml   # fails if a session has no corners
CONFIGS=configs/r3c.yaml GB_PER_CACHE=100 QUALITY=85 bash scripts/build_caches.sh 4
tail -f logs/build_caches.log                           # until "all caches done", 0 failed
python -m pianovam_vision.build_cache --config configs/r3c.yaml --check
python -m pianovam_vision.check_alignment --config configs/r3c.yaml --csv results/alignment_r3c.csv \
    > results/alignment_r3c.txt 2>&1; tail -n 15 results/alignment_r3c.txt

# 6. zero-shot PianoVAM -> R3 with the paper checkpoints (evaluations only). Run them
#    when NO training runs (RAM, see 7), e.g. between two queues. a = PianoVAM
#    thresholds, b = thresholds calibrated on R3 valid. KeyWise needs the hand-set
#    corners here too (r3c.yaml). Delete the outputs of any earlier attempt first:
#    rm -rf rolls/R3E3* out/R3E3*_test results/R3E3* logs/R3E3*
ls runs/vam_full_s2/best.pt runs/vam_full_s3/best.pt    # copy s3 from PHD/server/runs if missing
for s in 2 3; do
  DATA_CONFIG=configs/r3c.yaml OVERRIDES="keyboard.decode_height=1080" BASELINE_MIDI=$HOME/V2N_r3_test \
    THRESHOLDS_FROM=results/E1s${s}_vam_full_calibrate.txt \
    bash scripts/evaluate_run.sh R3E3a_s${s} configs/vam_full.yaml runs/vam_full_s${s}/best.pt full_f1
  DATA_CONFIG=configs/r3c.yaml OVERRIDES="keyboard.decode_height=1080" BASELINE_MIDI=$HOME/V2N_r3_test \
    bash scripts/evaluate_run.sh R3E3b_s${s} configs/vam_full.yaml runs/vam_full_s${s}/best.pt full_f1
done

# 7. THE BASELINE: KeyWise from scratch on R3 (r3c.yaml), 3 seeds, then PianoVAM -> R3
#    fine-tuning (~27 h per run, ~14 h per fine-tuning, ~6 days). Each training is
#    evaluated by itself (calibrate on valid, score test, compare with V2N per chunk).
#    EVAL_WAIT=1: the next training waits for that evaluation -- with ~10 GB of free RAM,
#    a training next to an evaluation left 1 GB (2026-11-18). The list starts with the two
#    official-box runs (r3_full done, r3_full_s2 trained/evaluated if not yet). A training
#    does not start while its cache is incomplete (run_experiment.sh checks).
EVAL_WAIT=1 BASELINE_MIDI=$HOME/V2N_r3_test bash scripts/queue.sh scripts/jobs_r3.txt
tail -f logs/queue_jobs_r3.log                          # stop: kill $(cat logs/queue_jobs_r3.pid)
tail -f runs/r3c_full/train.log                         # epochs; first check after epoch 1
bash scripts/status.sh

# 8. once r3c_full is trained: R3 -> PianoVAM zero-shot (thresholds from PianoVAM valid)
DATA_CONFIG=configs/vam_full.yaml OVERRIDES="keyboard.decode_height=720" \
  bash scripts/evaluate_run.sh R3E5b_r3c_full configs/r3c.yaml runs/r3c_full/best.pt full_f1

# 9. results (CPU): 3 runs vs V2N per subset, with and without the 10 out-of-sync chunks;
#    official boxes vs hand-set corners (same seeds)
R3E1=R3E1_r3c_full,R3E1s2_r3c_full,R3E1s3_r3c_full
for sub in r3s r3x; do
  python paper/compare_runs.py --results results --subset $sub --a $R3E1 --b V2N > results/R3E1_vs_V2N_$sub.txt
  python paper/compare_runs.py --results results --subset $sub \
      --a R3E1_r3c_full,R3E1s2_r3c_full --b R3E1_r3_full,R3E1s2_r3_full > results/R3E1_corners_vs_boxes_$sub.txt
done
NOSYNC=ls_2024-02-06_a02_split1,ls_2024-02-06_a02_split2,ls_2024-02-06_a02_split3,ls_2024-02-06_a02_split4,ls_2024-02-06_a02_split5,ls_2024-02-06_a02_split6,ls_2024-02-06_a03_split1,ls_2024-02-06_a03_split2,ls_2024-02-06_a03_split3,ls_2024-02-06_a03_split4
python paper/compare_runs.py --results results --subset r3x --exclude $NOSYNC \
    --a $R3E1 --b V2N > results/R3E1_vs_V2N_r3x_nosync.txt
for sub in r3s r3x; do
  python paper/compare_runs.py --results results --subset $sub \
      --a R3E4s2_r3c_finetune,R3E4s3_r3c_finetune --b R3E1s2_r3c_full,R3E1s3_r3c_full > results/R3E4_vs_R3E1_$sub.txt
done

# 10. only if the paper needs them: the paper-1 ablations on R3 (~11 days)
EVAL_WAIT=1 BASELINE_MIDI=$HOME/V2N_r3_test bash scripts/queue.sh scripts/jobs_r3_ablations.txt
#    then compare_runs as in 9 (velocity: --metrics velocity, A = R3E7*_r3c_vel)
```

Notes:
* One queue at a time; evaluations wait for each other by themselves.
* If step 2 shows other folder names (e.g. the zips were unpacked elsewhere), set
  `data.r3_split_dirs` in `configs/r3.yaml`; nothing else depends on the names.
* Training resumes from `last.pt` if anything stops: start the same queue again.
